# How to use these guides

## During a workshop

1. Open the pages your facilitator links (or start from [AI building](/ai-building/) and [Prompts](/prompts/)).
2. Copy prompts from fenced `text` blocks; replace `[brackets]` with your idea.
3. Use the sprint checklist mid-build; run the quality gate before you demo.

## For facilitators

- Put **reusable** teaching content in this library (`prompts/`, `ai-building/`, …).
- Put **day-only** material under `events/…` (public agenda and links).
- Do not publish private venue codes, internal ops, or unpublished commercial details here.

## Suggest a change

Use **Edit on GitHub** on any page, or open a PR in [Aischool-de/open-guide](https://github.com/Aischool-de/open-guide).
